Scene 1: The Warning & Mandate
Visual Animation: Noah standing under a dramatic, glowing sky. God's light shines down; clouds drift in parallax layers. Tapping Noah makes him nod and hold his wooden staff.
Narration Text: "God saw that the earth was full of trouble, but Noah walked with God. God said, 'Build an ark of wood, for a great flood is coming to renew the earth.'"


Scene 2: Building the Ark
Visual Animation: Time-lapse animation of timber framework raising up. Noah and his sons hammer planks into place. Interactive tap: children can tap wooden logs to trigger hammering and watch planks snap into place.
Narration Text: "Noah obeyed. He and his family chopped timber, shaped heavy planks, and sealed every seam with pitch to keep the water out." 


Scene 3: Animals Two by Two
Visual Animation: Parade of animal pairs walking into the ark door (elephants, giraffes, doves, lions). Tapping any animal triggers a bounce animation, walking movement, and distinct voice call.
Narration Text: "From every creature on earth, animals came two by two—male and female, bird and beast—marching safely into the ark."

Scene 4: The Great Flood
Visual Animation: Vector raindrops fall in layered particle rain. Dark storm clouds roll overhead while the ark sways smoothly on rising blue waves. Tapping the water creates splashing ripples.
Narration Text: "The rain fell for forty days and forty nights. Deep waters rose over the hills, and the ark floated safely above the flood."

Scene 5: The Dove & Land
Visual Animation: Noah opens a window atop the ark and releases a white dove. Dove glides across cloud layers and returns carrying an olive branch. Tapping dove makes it flap wings and fly faster.
Narration Text: "Noah sent out a dove to search for land. It returned carrying a fresh olive leaf in its beak—a sign that dry land had finally appeared."

Scene 6: The Rainbow Promise
Visual Animation: Ark resting on Mt. Ararat. Animals step out onto green grass. A vibrant multi-colored rainbow animates across the sky. Tapping rainbow triggers sparkling light particle effects.
Narration Text: "The doors opened and life returned to the earth. God placed a bright rainbow in the sky as a lasting promise of peace and hope."

Scene 7: Thanks be to God
Visual Animation: Noah and his famimly praying to God - "Dear God, thank You for always being with us. Help us trust You and be patient when we have to wait. Amen."
Narration Text: "We can trust God, even when we have to wait." Genesis 9:13 "I have set my rainbow in the clouds, and it will be the sign of the covenant between me and the earth.
